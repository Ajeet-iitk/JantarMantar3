Every obstacle costs you a flag — barricades, potholes, tear gas, officers. You get 3 flags; at zero the march ends. A water bottle restores one flag.
Collect fellow protestors — every one who joins your crowd stacks your score, and a clean streak multiplies it. Get as many to Jantar Mantar as you can.
Police lock-ups throw you onto a different Delhi street; the march carries on from where it stopped.
